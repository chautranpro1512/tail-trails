Why Tail Trails

A trained team, not a one-person gig

Trained before the first visit

Every caregiver completes 20 hours of training in pet behaviour, safe handling, Pet First Aid and reporting back to you.

Screened and insured

Criminal record checks, references and a three-month probation for new caregivers. The business carries liability insurance.

Backup when someone is sick

Trained caregivers on standby keep your booking on schedule.

Your pet stays home

Familiar smells, their own bed and no kennel stress, for dogs, cats and small pets alike.

Winter in Saskatchewan

−20 °C

Winter care, done properly

At −20 °C and colder we use coats and paw protection, shorten walks, and move play indoors when the wind chill is dangerous. Drop-ins also check that your home is warm and pipes are fine.

How we keep pets safe

  • Double-clipped leashes and GPS tracking on every walk
  • A hands-on assessment before we accept a new pet
  • Your vet’s details and emergency contact on file
  • A clear incident process, and we call you first

What we don’t do

  • Diagnose illness or give medical advice
  • Give complex prescription medication
  • Behaviour training or professional grooming
  • Walk dogs with an unassessed history of aggression

If your pet needs medical care, we contact you and the nearest vet clinic right away.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to be home?

No. We collect your key at the meet & greet and lock up after every visit.

What pets do you care for?

Dogs, cats and small pets such as rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, birds and fish. Leave your details in the form and we'll talk it through when we call.

Do you walk cats?

Cats don’t need walks, so we visit instead: food, fresh water, a clean litter box and play time.

How does overnight house sitting work?

A caregiver stays the night in your home so your pets keep their normal routine. Send a request with your dates and we'll give you a price.

What if my dog pulls, barks or doesn’t like other dogs?

Every new pet is assessed first. If we can’t care for your pet safely, we tell you and don’t take the booking.

Can you give my pet medication?

We don’t give complex prescription medication. Tell us what your pet needs at the meet & greet and we'll be clear about what we can and can’t do.

How fast will you reply?

We contact every request by the next business day, often the same day.

Where do you work?

Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan. Nearby towns may follow as the team grows.
